# EduLink — Product Description (Front to Back)

## Summary
EduLink is a school learning and management platform that brings students, teachers, and administrators into one connected space. It centralizes class materials, quizzes, announcements, and academic organization so that learning continues smoothly in and out of the classroom.

## Who It’s For
- **Students**: access subjects, download notes, take quizzes, and track progress.
- **Teachers**: share lesson materials, create and manage quizzes, and post subject updates.
- **Heads of Department (HODs)**: oversee subjects, teachers, and students in their departments.
- **Vice Principal / Principal**: manage the whole school structure, enroll users, and broadcast messages.

## What It Delivers
- **Learning Materials**: teachers upload notes and syllabi for students to access.
- **Online Quizzes**: timed multiple‑choice tests with instant results.
- **Announcements**: school‑wide, department, class, or subject messages.
- **Notifications**: alerts when new notes, quizzes, or announcements are posted.
- **Enrollment & IDs**: admins create student and teacher accounts with auto‑generated IDs.
- **Academic Structure**: domains, sub‑domains, departments, class levels, and subjects organized in one place.
- **Assignments & Curriculum Topics**: structured topics and tasks per subject.

## How It Works (Front to Back)
### 1) Entry & Access
- Users sign in to the EduLink portal.
- Each person sees a tailored experience based on their role.

### 2) Personalized Dashboards
- Students see their subjects, active quizzes, and recent announcements.
- Teachers see their subjects, students, notes, and quizzes.
- HODs and school leadership see department‑level or school‑level summaries.

### 3) Learning Materials Flow
- Teachers upload subject notes and syllabi.
- Students who are enrolled in those subjects instantly see the materials.
- New uploads generate notifications to the right learners.

### 4) Quiz & Assessment Flow
- Teachers create quizzes for specific subjects and set timing rules.
- Students take quizzes during the allowed window and get scores.
- Attempts are tracked so teachers can review outcomes and allow retakes if needed.

### 5) Announcements & Communication
- Admins, HODs, or teachers post announcements to the right audience.
- Students and staff receive these in their dashboard feed and notifications.

### 6) Academic Organization
- School leaders define domains, departments, class levels, and subjects.
- Teachers and students are assigned to the correct subjects and classes.
- This structure controls who sees which materials and updates.

### 7) Oversight & Visibility
- School leadership has a clear view of enrollment, subjects, and activity.
- Departments can monitor their own teachers, students, and subject performance.

## Typical User Journeys
### Student
1. Logs in and sees a personalized dashboard.
2. Opens a subject to access notes or take a quiz.
3. Receives notifications when something new is posted.

### Teacher
1. Logs in and sees their classes and subject list.
2. Uploads notes, creates quizzes, and posts announcements.
3. Reviews student quiz attempts and progress.

### HOD / Leadership
1. Logs in to review department or school‑wide summaries.
2. Manages subjects, teachers, and students.
3. Sends announcements to targeted groups.

## Value for Schools
- **Consistency**: all learning content and communication in one place.
- **Clarity**: each role sees only what matters to them.
- **Speed**: materials and announcements reach students immediately.
- **Accountability**: activity and results are tracked transparently.
